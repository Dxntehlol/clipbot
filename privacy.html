ClipBot – Privacy Policy

ClipBot is a personal, single-user tool. It only ever acts on the accounts of its owner.

  • What it stores: sign-in tokens for the owner's own social media accounts, and the owner's own video clips. These are kept on the owner's computer.
  • What it does not do: it does not collect, store, sell, or share any information about other people, and it does not track visitors to this page.
  • Third parties: videos are uploaded only to the platforms the owner chooses (for example TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), under those platforms' own privacy policies.
  • Deleting data: the owner can revoke access at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings, and delete stored tokens and clips from the computer.

Questions: contact the account owner through the connected social media account.
